Shared-settings study (integration branch `integration/shared-pinn-settings`, Prem, 31 Oct 2026; proposed, needs Prem and Rohan to confirm): what limits inverse recovery. (1) Rohan's final WP05 settings recover **0 of 18** clean fits on fresh seeds. The main cause is the optimiser budget: his L-BFGS runs 200 iterations and stops on tolerances (ftol 1e-12, gtol 1e-8); with 2500 iterations and no tolerance stop the same trainer recovers the well-conditioned cases (A regular, B regular, A extinction) to 1e-3 to 1e-5. (2) A near-fold, B near-fold and B decline are practically unidentifiable on the 60% training window even with clean data: the data are fitted almost perfectly while parameters are off by 40% to 100%, the Jacobian condition numbers are 1e3 to 2e4 (62 to 340 for the recoverable cases), and in the near-fold cases r and A both collapse to about 1e-6 along a valley that keeps r/A (the cubic production term is unchanged). SciPy ODE least squares recovers all six cases to 1e-5 to 1e-8, so they are identifiable in principle; the PINN's data fit (1e-4 to 1e-5) is not tight enough for the condition number. No hyperparameter fixes this; parameter bounds or a different parametrisation would be needed. (3) Selection stage (seeds 0/1/42, 8 candidates planned, 64-wide networks dropped by the owner's rule): the network learning rate is the dominant factor (1e-3 beats 1e-2); tanh 32x32 lr 1e-3 and swish 32x32 lr 1e-3 tie at score 28. The inverse-only knobs matter little (lambda_phys 0.1 and lr_params 1e-2 tie for best). (4) Confirmation on fresh seeds: both `tanh 32x32 lr 1e-3` and `swish 32x32 lr 1e-2` are perfect on the forward problem and recover 8 and 11 of 18 clean inverse fits; they are statistically indistinguishable (pooled 18/36 each); noisy recovery can only be judged for A regular and B regular at sigma 0.01 (all other combinations have Cramer-Rao bounds above 25%). Proposed single setting: tanh, two hidden layers of 32, network learning rate 1e-3, 300 collocation points, Adam 1000 then L-BFGS 2500 with no tolerance stop, lambda_phys 0.1, lr_params 1e-3 (the stage-2 tie-break; 1e-2 is equally defensible). Consequence for WP04 (PR #6): its documented choice of swish with lr 1e-2 was made comparing activations at lr 1e-2 only; at lr 1e-3 tanh is at least as good on the forward problem, so PR #6 and the final shared setting currently disagree. Not yet applied to `configs/wp04_final.json` or `configs/wp05_final.json`.

Noise and convergence findings (integration branch `integration/shared-pinn-settings`, Prem, 31 Oct 2026; proposed, needs Prem and Rohan to confirm): at noise sigma 0.001, 0.005 and 0.01 (and clean) the inverse optimiser reaches the noise floor in 132 of 144 fits with Rohan's default trainer; the 12 that do not all come from validation-based early stopping and checkpoint selection (they end `validation_stopped` with parameters still at their starting guess). A new optional `validation_selection=False` switch in `InversePINNTrainer` (default True = unchanged, covered by a unit test) removes them: all 144 fits then reach the noise floor. It does not raise parameter recovery overall, because the remaining failures are structural (A near-fold, B near-fold and B decline are practically unidentifiable on the training window; r and A can collapse together along a valley) and do not depend on the noise level. Recommendation: use `validation_selection=False` with the shared setting (tanh, 2 x 32, network lr 1e-3, 300 points, Adam 1000 + L-BFGS 2500, lambda_phys 0.1, lr_params 1e-3); keep it as an explicit option so WP05 can still use validation-based selection where a held-out window is informative. Tanh is at least as good as swish at every noise level.

Shared setting adopted (integration branch `integration/shared-pinn-settings`, Prem, 31 Oct 2026; for Prem and Rohan to confirm in review): the forward and inverse problems both use tanh, two hidden layers of 32, network learning rate 1e-3, 300 collocation points, hard initial condition, Adam 1000 then L-BFGS 2500 with no tolerance stop; the inverse problem additionally uses lambda_phys 0.1, lr_params 1e-3 and `validation_selection=False`. Results: forward 18/18 on both seed sets; inverse clean 10/18 (was 0/18). WP04's report/notebook text and WP05's report/notebook text still describe the previous settings and must be updated before merge.
